Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Introduction

Freqalto ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our Freqalto mobile application (the "App").

Information We Do NOT Collect

No microphone or audio recording
No personal information (name, email, phone)
No location data
No access to contacts or photos

Freqalto generates audio signals synthetically. We do NOT access your microphone or record any audio from your device.

Information Collected Automatically

When you use our App, we may collect certain information automatically through third-party services:

📢 Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements in the App. AdMob may collect:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
  • Usage Data: How you interact with ads (impressions, clicks)
  • IP Address: Used to determine general geographic location (country/region level)

For more information about Google's data practices, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information

💳 In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

We offer in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. When you make a purchase, Google may collect:

  • Purchase Information: Transaction records, purchase tokens for verification
  • Google Account Information: Used to verify purchases and enable restoration across devices
  • Payment Details: Processed securely by Google; we never receive or store your payment information

Available in-app purchases:

  • PRO: Removes advertisements and unlocks MP3 and PCM export
  • Best Value: Includes everything in PRO, plus 30+ presets, saving and sharing your own presets, and 7 exclusive themes
  • Upgrade to Best Value: Lets PRO owners upgrade to Best Value at a reduced price

For more information, visit: Google Payments Privacy Notice

🔐 Consent Management (Google User Messaging Platform)

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to collect and manage your advertising consent in compliance with GDPR and other privacy regulations. This service:

  • Presents consent dialogs to users in applicable regions (e.g., EU/EEA)
  • Stores your consent preferences securely
  • Does not collect additional personal data beyond consent status

📊 Diagnostics & Performance

The App does not include any third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Diagnostic and performance data (such as app launch time and interaction events) may be collected automatically by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above, and Google Play may provide us aggregated crash reports if you have opted in to sharing usage and diagnostics on your device.

🛠️ Technical Diagnostic Log (on your device)

To help fix problems such as a store connection that does not respond, the App keeps up to 5 recent sessions of a technical diagnostic log in its private storage on your device. Logging runs automatically for the first 24 hours and then stops; it can be switched on again for another 24 hours from a hidden tool in the About screen. It contains technical events only (app version, device model, Android version, consent status and timings of store and ad requests), and no name, email, account identifier, purchase token or audio. The log is not sent anywhere automatically. It leaves your device only if you choose to share it, for example when contacting support. You can clear it at any time, and uninstalling the App deletes it.

In applicable regions (such as the EEA and UK), when you first open the App you will be presented with a consent dialog (GDPR-compliant) that allows you to:
